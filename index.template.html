Our story

Small kitchen. Big heart.
Everything made when you order it.

Piccolino means "the little one" — and that's the whole idea. A small kitchen on Korça's pedonale doing a few things really well: dough made fresh every day, real ingredients picked with care, and every order given full attention from the first ingredient to the moment it lands in your hands.

— Piccolino, Bulevardi Shën Gjergji, Korçë
